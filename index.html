mycourses
Privacy

Narrated lessons, made for one learner

mycourses is a personal, non-commercial app for reading and listening to lessons that Claude writes on request.

Each lesson is a single .lesson file: readable pages with paragraphs read aloud by the device's own voice, pictures, small interactive exercises and quizzes. The app runs on Android and on a Windows PC, and works offline.

Google Drive

Lessons are shared from the PC to the phone through the user's own Google Drive. When the user signs in with Google, the app can only see the lesson and progress files it created itself. It cannot see any other file in the Drive.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

mycourses is a personal app with no servers of its own. It collects nothing about you and sends nothing to its developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your devices

The lessons you open, your reading position, quiz answers, review cards, and how you organise lessons into courses and tags are stored only on the device where you use the app. Reading aloud uses your device's built-in voice.

Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, the app asks only for the drive.file permission. With it, the app can:

  • create lesson files and progress files in your Google Drive,
  • list, download and update those same files.

It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. The files go straight between your device and Google; they never pass through any other server. The app does not use your Google data for advertising, does not sell or share it, and does not use it to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can sign out in the app, or remove its access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes everything it stored on the device. Files it created in your Drive stay there until you delete them.
